Built for the way data actually leaks

Your data never leaves your network

Fully self-hosted on Kubernetes. No SaaS round trip, no vendor cloud, no telemetry carrying record content. A default-deny network policy limits each pod to your brokers.

Accurate on structured data

Every JSON or XML record is parsed and redacted by field name and by content, so "LastName": "Smith" is caught even though the value matches no pattern.

Fails closed

A record that cannot be processed goes to an access-restricted dead-letter topic, never to the clean one. Exactly-once delivery when you need it.

Change policy without holding your breath

1 · Sign

Rules that cannot be quietly weakened

Redaction rules ship as signed policy bundles. A connector obeys only a trusted signature and a newer version; a forged or rolled-back bundle changes nothing.

Signed policy bundles

2 · Shadow

See the effect before it applies

A candidate policy runs beside the live one on real traffic. You get the difference as counts, by data type, and nobody has to look at the data.

Shadow mode

3 · Prove

Evidence an auditor can check

Content-free audit events record what was removed, under which policy, in a hash chain that shows any event altered, removed or reordered.

Audit events

Measured, not estimated

Throughput per core, end-to-end numbers through Kafka and where the time goes are published with the script that produced them. Run it on your own hardware.
